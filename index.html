Each mechanic below was studied from the open-source component scene, then rebuilt from scratch in the studio's language. Same ideas, new bones.

Streaming reply

Words land one beat at a time behind a quiet caret, with the key phrase surfacing as it forms.

Hold-to-think

How long you hold send sets how hard the model works. The ring fills, the status line narrates, release commits.

Model picker

A pill that unfolds into a quiet list instead of a dropdown menu. The dot recolours to the tier you pick.

Inline status

Reading, scanning, drafting, polishing. A working assistant shows its hands while it works.

Source pills

Citations arrive as small pills above the answer, not footnotes under it.

Slide-out menu

The menu never sits still: a glass drawer glides in from the edge, links tumbling in one beat apart.

Fluid orb

A canvas metaball field with drifting shading. It leans toward your pointer, gently, never glaringly.

Liquid glass

Chrome takes the iPhone treatment: layered translucency, a lit top edge, depth instead of flat pills.

Waveform voice

A scrubbable voice bar: drag to seek, tap to play. The demo speaks in silence, on purpose.
